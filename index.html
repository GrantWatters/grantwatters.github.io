About

My first career was spent reading what was actually happening on the ground and fixing it before it became a bigger problem — coordinating film sets, running a recirculating aquaculture site back to full production, supervising groundworks crews, and responding to emergencies as a retained firefighter. Every one of those roles came down to the same thing: accurate reporting, clear communication with people who didn't have my technical background, and taking ownership when something needed fixing.

I'm now doing that same work with data instead of machinery. My training has taken me through the full analysis workflow — cleaning and structuring data in SQL and spreadsheets, running exploratory and statistical analysis, and building dashboards in Tableau and Matplotlib that hold up on their own for a non-technical audience.
